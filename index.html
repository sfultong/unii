unii

unii is a personal assistant program that one person runs on their own computer. It is a tree of AI agents, each with one job and one long-running chat, driven by a language model that also runs on that computer.

One of those agents reads its owner's recent email through the Gmail API, read-only, so it can summarize mail and point out what needs attention. unii is not a service, has no accounts, and is not offered to the public: the only person who signs in to it is the person running it.

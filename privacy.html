unii privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

unii is a personal program that runs on its user's own computer (see the home page). This policy describes what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

With your permission, unii uses the Gmail API with the gmail.readonly scope to read the email in your account from roughly the past day, excluding spam and trash: each message's headers, labels, the names of its attachments, and its text. unii cannot send, change, or delete email.

How it uses that data

The email is given to an AI agent running on your computer, so it can summarize your mail and tell you what needs attention. The language model that reads it also runs on your computer. unii does not use your data for advertising, for training AI models, or for anything other than showing it back to you.

Where it is stored

Email that unii reads is stored only on your computer, in unii's local database. The OAuth tokens that let unii read your mail are stored only on your computer, in a file readable only by your user account.

Sharing

unii does not send your data to its developer or to any third party, and does not sell or share it. It has no analytics or tracking. Its only network connections for email are to Google's own servers.

Your control

You can stop unii's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections (Security, then third-party connections). You can delete everything unii has stored by deleting its database and its files in ~/.config/unii.

Google API Services User Data Policy

unii's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/sfultong/unii/issues.
